# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Cap'nShare welcomes contributions and discussion from people with different backgrounds, identities, and experience levels. We want project spaces to remain respectful, inclusive, accessible, and focused on improving the work.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, patient, and considerate.
- Critique code and ideas without attacking people.
- Explain disagreements clearly and listen to feedback.
- Respect privacy and do not publish personal information or private communications without consent.
- Keep discussion relevant and help make project spaces usable for newcomers.
- Discuss food-sharing and safety concerns with care and without shaming people.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, personal attacks, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing, deliberate disruption, and sharing private information without consent are not acceptable. Repeatedly derailing discussion or using demeaning language is also unacceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, and other spaces when someone is representing or participating in the Cap'nShare project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report a conduct concern to the maintainer, Brajesh Kumar (@brajesh31), through the contact options on https://github.com/brajesh31. Provide enough context to identify the incident. Do not include sensitive information in public issues.

The maintainer will review reports fairly and as promptly as practical, protect reporter privacy where possible, and may ask for more context. Depending on the circumstances, responses may include a warning, content removal, temporary restrictions, or exclusion from project spaces. The maintainer will communicate outcomes when appropriate.
